Delete Your Account and Data

Last updated: 23 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your DoomBlock account and the data linked to it. It applies to the DoomBlock app published by Bros Dolz LLC for Android.

Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open DoomBlock and go to Settings.
  2. Tap your account (the row showing your email address).
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

What gets deleted: your account and your cloud backup - your email or login, your block groups (the apps, websites, keywords and feeds you chose), schedules, session limits, settings, cooldown configuration, the record of blocks you overrode, and the stored hash of your PIN. The app on your device is also reset to a fresh install. This happens right away and cannot be undone.

Your subscription is separate. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription and does not refund it. Cancel it yourself in the Play Store, under Payments & subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own policy.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot use the app, email support@brosdolz.com from the address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will check that the request belongs to you and delete your account and cloud backup within 30 days.

Data retention

Once deleted, your account data is not kept. Anonymous grouped data that cannot identify you may still remain outside your account. Records that Google Play holds about your purchase are kept by Google under its own policy and are not ours to delete.

For the full picture of what DoomBlock collects and why, see our Privacy Policy.
