Delete Your Account and Data

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your DoomBlock account and the data linked to it. It applies to the DoomBlock app published by Bros Dolz LLC for Android.

First: which kind of account do you have?

Signing in to DoomBlock is optional, and which route below applies to you depends on whether you did. Open DoomBlock, go to Settings, and look under Account:

  • A row showing your email address (or "Signed in with Google") — you have an account you signed into. Use Delete from inside the app.
  • "Add an account", with a note that you are using DoomBlock without one — you have an account with no email address attached. It was created when you continued without signing in, and it holds a cloud backup of your setup even though you never gave us an email. Use If you are using DoomBlock without an account.

Both kinds have a cloud copy of your setup, so both are worth deleting if that is what you want. The difference is only in how you prove the account is yours.

Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open DoomBlock and go to Settings.
  2. Tap your account (the row showing your email address).
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm it. If you have set a PIN you will be asked for it first, then to type your email address and the word goodbye — deliberate friction, because the deletion cannot be undone.

What gets deleted: your account and your cloud backup — your email or login and the ID behind it, your block groups (the apps, websites, keywords and short-form content you chose), schedules, session limits, cooldown configuration, app preferences, your statistics counters, your override history with its timestamps, how you labelled apps as productive or distracting, your subscription record, any beta code you redeemed, and the stored hash of your PIN. The app on your device is also reset to a fresh install. This happens right away and cannot be undone.

Your subscription is separate. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription and does not refund it. Cancel it yourself in the Play Store, under Payments & subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own policy.

If you are using DoomBlock without an account

Your account has no email address on it, so there is no account row to tap and no address for us to match a request against. There are two ways to get the data deleted, and one thing to avoid.

1. Add an email address, then delete (fastest, and entirely in your hands)

Go to Settings → Add an account and add an email address or your Google account. This attaches to the account you already have rather than creating a new one — the same ID, the same backup, the same subscription — so nothing is duplicated and nothing is lost. The account row then appears in Settings, and the steps above delete everything, immediately.

The address is used to verify the deletion and then it goes with the rest of the account. If you would rather not give one, use the next route.

2. Ask us to delete it

Email support@brosdolz.com with the subject "Delete my account" and tell us you were using DoomBlock without an account. So that we delete the right record and not someone else's, include whichever of these applies:

  • If you subscribed: your Google Play order number (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or the receipt Google emailed you). Your purchase is stored against the account, so the order number is enough for us to find it.
  • If you redeemed a beta or comp code: the code and roughly when you redeemed it. If that code was issued to you alone, it identifies your record.

We will confirm what we found before deleting anything, and complete the deletion within 30 days of agreeing that the record is yours.

Being straight with you about the limit: if you never subscribed and never redeemed a code, an account with no email contains nothing that identifies you — no email, no name, no phone number, no device identifier — so we genuinely cannot tell which record is yours, and we will not delete a stranger's data on a guess. In that situation use route 1 while you still have the app installed, because it is the only one that can reach your account.

Do this before you uninstall

An account with no email is reachable only from the app on your phone. Uninstalling DoomBlock, or clearing its data, removes everything held on the device — but it also throws away the only key to that account, and the cloud backup then stays in our database out of reach of both of us (unless there is a purchase to trace it by, as above). So delete the account, or add an email address to it, before you uninstall.

Request deletion without the app (accounts with an email)

If you cannot use the app, email support@brosdolz.com from the address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will check that the request belongs to you and delete your account and cloud backup within 30 days.

Data retention

Once deleted, your account data is not kept. Anonymous grouped data that cannot identify you may still remain outside your account. An account with no email address that we cannot connect to a person is kept until it is deleted through one of the routes above. Records that Google Play holds about your purchase are kept by Google under its own policy and are not ours to delete.
